# FAST Agent Gallery
*Agent Observability, for Everyone*

The biggest obstacle to AI adoption is a mis-alignment between the owners of business opportunities and the owners of enterprise controls. *FAST Agent Gallery* resolves that conflict by empowering teams to take ownership of their own AI solutions while preserving enterprise governance.

## Vision
The people closest to your customers and business operations need clear visibility and ownership over their own AI solutions. To apply new AI capabilities to real business problems, they need answers to many new questions: *What AI agents can I use? Are they effective? Are they governed? What do they cost? Can they access data? Can they take actions? Do they learn? How can I experiment, and then expand, and then scale?*

Yet in many enterprises, these folks are stuck waiting. While start-ups and big tech adopt new ways of working,  established enterprises risk falling behind. Your business people have identified use cases and maybe worked with a partner on a prototype. Yet many are just waiting. *Waiting for approvals. Waiting for access. Waiting for training. Waiting for centralized governance.*

What's Needed? A more federated approach to AI Observability and Governance. A way for people across your org to move faster, safely. And for them to learn along the way, and share those learnings with the larger enterprise. Instead of building the perfect centralized data and AI strategy, leaders are shifting-left by putting more observability and governance into the hands of business and operations leaders.
